Staying alive

Hunger and thirst drop all the time. Cuts bleed until you bandage them. A bite infects you — antibiotics slow it.

The way out

At 02:00 a rescue helicopter lands at the crossroads of Main & Maple. It waits one minute. Be there, and press E at its door. Miss it, and there's one more, the next night. The last one.

People

The living are people, not targets. Hurt one and they fight back or run — and everyone who saw it stops trusting you.

The dead

They see you in front of them — far in daylight, barely at night (unless your flashlight's on). They hear you run, shoot, smash and open doors. Gunshots bring the whole street. Close a door behind you: they'll have to beat it down. Nights are worse: more of them, and some of them run.
